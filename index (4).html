The Task

Descriptive Essay

Imagine you are standing on the deck of a big submarine just before it dives into the deep blue sea. Write a descriptive essay of about 150–200 words telling your reader exactly what the submarine looks like, sounds like, and feels like. Use your five senses so your reader can picture it clearly.

🧭

Writing Guide & Structure

Follow these four parts to organise your essay.

1

Introduction

Tell what you are describing and where you saw it.

2

Body

Describe the outside first (shape, color, conning tower), then the inside (portholes, dials, crew).

3

Conclusion

Say how you feel about the submarine and the adventure ahead.
